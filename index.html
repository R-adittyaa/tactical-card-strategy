📖 HOW TO PLAY

🎯

TUJUAN

Hancurin King musuh (HP 20) sebelum King lu dihancurin.

💰

GOLD

+5 gold per turn. Interest +1 per 10 gold disimpen. Beli kartu di shop.

🛒

SHOP

5 kartu random muncul tiap turn. Beli = cost × 2. Roll = -2G.

⚡

ACTION POINTS

AP nambah tiap turn (max 10). Buat main kartu, gerak, attack.

🎮

CARA MAIN

Beli kartu → main ke board → gerak/attack → END TURN.
